Product Specification: Course Hub & Study Notes Platform1. Executive SummaryCourse Hub is a collaborative, peer-to-peer educational platform that connects students taking the same classes. The app allows users to share study notes, upload course files, and publish studying methods. Content accessibility is strictly scoped by class enrollment—students only see and share study materials related to the specific courses they are currently taking.2. Key Features & Functionality2.1 Enrollment-Based Access & OnboardingSchool & Institution Selection: Users select their university, college, or high school during onboarding.Class Enrollment: Users search for and add specific course sections to their profile (e.g., CS 101: Intro to CS - Fall 2026).Dynamic Feed Scoping: The user's main dashboard automatically filters notes, files, and discussion posts based on their active class enrollments.2.2 Targeted File Upload & Material SharingClass-Specific Uploads: When uploading a file (PDF, DOCX, PNG, slides, zip), the uploader selects target class(es). Only students enrolled in those selected classes can view or download the files.Resource Categorization: Uploaders label their content with categories:Lecture NotesExam Prep / Review GuidesPractice Problems & SolutionsFlashcard Decks & Cheat SheetsLab Reports / Syllabus ResourcesFile Preview & Annotation: Built-in PDF and image previewer allowing users to read materials directly within the app.2.3 Study Methods & Techniques HubMethod Sharing: A dedicated section where students post proven learning strategies (e.g., active recall schedules, Pomodoro setups, Feynman technique applications).General vs. Course-Specific Methods: Methods can be posted generally across the platform or tagged to a specific tough course (e.g., "How to study for Organic Chemistry II").Peer Upvoting & Feedback: Users upvote effective study techniques and leave comments on how well a strategy worked for them.2.4 Search, Filtering & BookmarksIn-Class Search: Search through uploaded materials within a specific class using keyword search on titles, tags, and descriptions.Saved Library: Users can bookmark resources and study methods to quickly access them offline or prior to exams.3. Security, Content Moderation & Academic IntegrityAccess Control Middleware: Ensure backend checks that a requesting user is actively enrolled in course_id before serving any restricted file URLs.Academic Integrity Policies: Require users to agree to honor code guidelines prohibiting the sharing of live exam questions, graded assignments, or copyrighted textbook scans.Report & Moderation System: Enable students to flag inappropriate uploads or copyright infringements for admin review.File Virus & Malware Scanning: Integrate automated scanning (e.g., AWS GuardDuty / ClamAV) on uploaded files prior to making them accessible.8. Development RoadmapPhase 1 (MVP): Authentication, course creation/enrollment, basic file uploading (PDF/Image) to specific courses, and class-scoped feed.Phase 2: Study Methods section with rich text formatting, upvoting system, and in-app file previewer.Phase 3: Push notifications for new uploads in enrolled classes, bookmarking, and advanced search filters.
